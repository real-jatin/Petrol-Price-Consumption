⛽ Petrol Price & Consumption Analysis | SQL Project
📌 Short Description / Purpose

A SQL-based analytical project designed to study petrol price trends across Indian states from 2005–2024. The project analyzes the impact of crude oil prices, taxation policies, COVID-19, and regional VAT structures on fuel pricing patterns.

🛠️ Tech Stack

• 🗄️ MySQL – Database creation and querying
• 🧠 SQL Queries – Data analysis and trend extraction
• 🔗 Relational Database Modeling – Multiple linked tables for states, crude prices, and tax events
• 📁 Structured Data Management – Data cleaning, constraints, and validations

📂 Data Source

Custom structured dataset containing:

State-wise petrol prices
Crude oil prices
Tax policy changes
COVID period indicators
VAT & transport cost indexes
🌟 Features & Highlights
🔴 Business Problem

Fuel prices in India vary significantly due to crude oil fluctuations, taxation policies, and regional factors, making trend analysis difficult without structured data.

🎯 Goal of the Project

To build a relational SQL database that analyzes:

Petrol price trends over time
State-wise fuel price differences
Crude oil impact on retail fuel prices
Effects of taxation and COVID-19 on pricing
📊 Key Analysis Performed
State-wise petrol price comparison
Crude oil vs average petrol price analysis
COVID-period fuel price impact
Tax policy and VAT analysis
Year-over-year petrol price trend analysis
📈 Business Insights
High VAT states consistently showed higher petrol prices
COVID-19 period caused major pricing anomalies
Retail petrol prices remained high despite crude oil fluctuations
Tax policies had a significant effect on fuel pricing trends
🚀 Business Impact

Helps understand fuel pricing behavior, taxation impact, and regional pricing differences using structured SQL analysis and relational database concepts.
